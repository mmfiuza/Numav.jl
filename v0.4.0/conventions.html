Conventions

Complex exponential signs

The sign convention for complex acoustical equations is $+i\omega t$. Thus, sound pressure at some point in space could be represented as $p(t) = \mathrm{Re} \{ \tilde{P} e^{i\omega t} \}$, where $\tilde{P}$ is the complex pressure amplitude.

Direction of known normal velocity conditions

To define a known normal velocity condition using add_sound_source!, the velocity is considered positive when pointing inwards, to the direction of the simulated domain's interior.

Direction of known specific acoustic impedence

Specific acoustic impedance of some surface is defined by

\[\tilde{Z} = \frac{\tilde{P}}{\tilde{U}}\]

where:

  • $ \tilde{P} $ is the complex pressure amplitude;
  • $ \tilde{U} $ is the complex normal velocity amplitude.

Given that, when defining a known specific acoustic impedance using add_surface_material!, the sign convention of the normal velocity is considered positive when pointing outwards, to the direction away from the simulated domain.
